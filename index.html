Got Questions?

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers to help you feel confident before your first visit to Simmi's studio.

How long do nail extensions typically last?

▼
With our professional Russian cuticle prep and premium adhesive techniques, our acrylic and soft gel extensions easily last between 3 to 4 weeks without lifting or chipping. If your natural nails grow fast, you can simply drop by for an infill/refill at week 3!

Will getting nail extensions damage my natural nails?

▼
No! Natural nails are only damaged when aggressive e-filing is done or when clients peel off their extensions at home. At Nail & Glow Studio, we never over-buff the natural nail plate, and we offer safe, gentle soak-off removal followed by deep keratin conditioning.

Can I show you a picture from Pinterest or Instagram?

▼
Yes, we absolutely encourage it! Bring any screenshot or video from Pinterest or Instagram. Simmi will assess the shape, color palette, and stones to recreate the exact look on your natural hands.

Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?

▼
Walk-ins are always welcomed! However, because nail extensions and intricate nail art take 60 to 90 minutes of dedicated hand craftsmanship, we strongly recommend booking a slot in advance (especially on Friday to Sunday) via WhatsApp or phone to avoid any waiting time.

Is Nail & Glow Studio a family / unisex salon?

▼
Yes! We are proud to be a welcoming, clean family salon in Phase 11. We offer hair spa, manicures, pedicures, and grooming services for all family members in a warm, respectful environment.
